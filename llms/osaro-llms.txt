## What OSARO sells

- Robotic piece picking — machine-learning software automating each-picking for distribution centers.
- Robotic bagging — automated bagging for high-SKU-variability e-commerce fulfillment.
- Robotic kitting — automated multi-item kit assembly, including subscription-box workflows.
- Mixed-case depalletizing — handling variable packaging, sizes and orientations on unstable pallets.
- SightWorks — the proprietary real-time perception platform underneath all of the above; also
  licensed to third parties (IHI licensed SightWorks for depalletizing in Japan).
- AutoModel — on-the-fly learning of new products without SKU pre-training or line interruption.
- HyperCare — OSARO's end-to-end deployment support program.
- Fleet — multi-station and enterprise-wide deployment scaling.

## Integration model

Integration is a professional-services engagement, not an API. OSARO systems interoperate with
existing warehouse estates — conveyors, ASRS, AMRs and warehouse management systems — and OSARO
partners with system integrators and automation providers for end-to-end delivery. Prospective
integrators go through https://www.osaro.com/contact. Pricing is quote-only: /pricing 301s to /contact.
